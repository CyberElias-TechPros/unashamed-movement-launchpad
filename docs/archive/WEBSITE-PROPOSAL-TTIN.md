## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The TTIN website is a comprehensive, high-impact digital platform designed for outreach, engagement, and growth for The Seventh Man Movement. This proposal outlines the complete development of a full-featured church/organization website with e-commerce capabilities, content management system, video integration, and interactive features.

---

## 2. FEATURES & FUNCTIONALITY

### 2.1 Core Website Pages
- **Home Page** - Hero video, testimonials slider, impact statistics (locations, countries), newsletter signup
- **About Page** - Origin story, plane preaching video, movement history
- **Testimonies Page** - Written testimonies, video testimonials, interactive country map showing preacher statistics
- **Merch (Shop) Page** - 24+ products across multiple categories with e-commerce functionality
- **Unashamed POD Page** - YouTube podcast structure, Instagram shorts grid
- **Resources Page** - Books and downloadable materials with working download links
- **Contact Page** - WhatsApp integration, email, Instagram links

### 2.2 Advanced Features
- **Responsive Design** - Mobile, tablet, and desktop optimization
- **Multiple Layout Systems** - Original and Sympos layouts with toggle functionality
- **Admin Panel** - Content management system for all dynamic content
- **Database Integration** - For testimonials, statistics, downloads tracking
- **Video Content System** - Hero videos, preaching videos, Instagram video integration
- **Newsletter System** - Email subscription and management
- **E-commerce Integration** - Merch purchases with payment processing
- **Interactive Map** - Country statistics visualization
- **Search Functionality** - For resources and testimonies
- **Analytics Integration** - User engagement tracking
- **Event Listings** - Event management and display
- **Media Gallery** - Video and image gallery
- **Contact Forms** - Multiple form types for inquiries

### 2.3 Technical Features
- **Real-time Updates** - Dynamic content management
- **File Upload System** - Video and resource management
- **Performance Optimization** - Lazy loading, caching, adaptive video streaming
- **Security Implementation** - CSP headers, input validation, error tracking
